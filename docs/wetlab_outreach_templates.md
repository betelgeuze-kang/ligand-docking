# Wet-Lab Outreach Templates

## Goal

These templates are designed to start a small pilot collaboration. The framing is intentionally narrow. We are not asking someone to validate an entire platform. We are asking for one bounded assay packet with predefined controls.

## Rules For Outreach

- lead with one target, not the whole roadmap
- mention that the compound list and controls are already prepared
- make the ask small enough to answer quickly
- offer either paid pilot or co-authored collaboration
- attach one target packet, not a long deck

## Template A: CRO / Paid Pilot

Subject: Small pilot assay request for `EGFR_KINASE`, `ADRB2_GPCR_BLIND`, or `HIV1_PROTEASE`

Hello,

We are preparing a small external validation pilot around a computational target-ranking workflow and are looking for a partner to run a tightly scoped assay package.

We already have:

- a fixed target choice
- a short control panel
- a predefined success criterion

For the first pilot, we are considering:

- `EGFR_KINASE` with a biochemical kinase assay, or
- `ADRB2_GPCR_BLIND` with an antagonist-mode `beta-arrestin` or `cAMP` assay, or
- `HIV1_PROTEASE` with a fluorogenic protease inhibition assay

The initial ask is intentionally small:

- 6 compounds total
- 3 expected positives
- 3 expected negatives
- duplicate or triplicate technical replicates

If this is a fit for your platform, we would like a rough quote, assay recommendation, and expected turnaround time.

We can share the target packet and control list immediately.

Best,

[name]
