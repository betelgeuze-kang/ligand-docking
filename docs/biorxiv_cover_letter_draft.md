# Draft Cover Letter For bioRxiv Submission

Dear Editors,

We are submitting the manuscript entitled `Preregistered Cross-Domain Blind Validation of a Unified Molecular Architecture` for consideration as a bioRxiv preprint.

This manuscript presents a preregistered and reviewer-auditable computational validation package for a unified molecular architecture evaluated across four domain classes: GPCR ligand ranking, ion-channel ligand ranking, kinase/protease prioritization, and release-grade intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) assessment. Rather than presenting a single favorable benchmark snapshot, the submission preserves the full corrective history, preregistered acceptance logic, baseline-gauntlet comparison, audited reviewer-facing package, and frozen current bundle.

The accepted current package (`2026-03-22_biorxiv_v7r1`) passes all three preregistered claim layers: the primary `Core Blind Set`, the secondary `Expanded OOD Set`, and the reproducibility-support `Operational Smoke Set`. The current bundle also includes a temporal-validation scaffold with explicit provenance coverage and policy-coded residual gaps. At the current freeze point, `202/206` rows are item-ready and the remaining `4/206` rows are explicitly classified rather than left as an unstructured limitation.

We believe the manuscript will be of interest to readers working on machine learning for molecular ranking and validation methodology because it emphasizes preregistration, claim-layer separation, auditable correction history, and cross-domain evaluation under frozen rules. The work is positioned as a strong computational validation package and does not claim prospective wet-lab hit discovery or experimental therapeutic validation.
